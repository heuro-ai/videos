---
name: character-continuity
description: Preserve a creator's approved appearance, voice, pronunciation and performance across video shots and versions.
---

Use the verified own avatar/voice IDs or authorized reference assets, plus the approved character profile. Record hair, clothing, background, camera framing, lighting, accent, delivery and key pronunciations. A supplied name is a lookup target, not a verified ID. Make a short test when identity or language changes and inspect face, mouth sync, pronunciation and gestures. Do not silently use a stock avatar when the requested personal identity is unavailable. Keep the same reference set across takes, and version any deliberate character change. Store original face and voice material privately.
