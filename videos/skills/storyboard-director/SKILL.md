---
name: storyboard-director
description: Convert an approved script into timed shots and tool-specific visual instructions.
---

For each shot record timing, narration, subject/action, camera, environment, lighting, audio, reference assets and exact on-screen text. Route speaking performance to a verified avatar, illustrative scenes to the selected generator, real UI to actual product captures, and exact graphics to composition tools. Respect current provider duration and aspect limitations. Keep camera movements achievable within each shot; design continuity across cuts. Composite labels after generation. Mark simulated aerials/maps/UI as illustrative in provenance. Deliver the storyboard, prompts, shot dependencies and test-shot plan.
