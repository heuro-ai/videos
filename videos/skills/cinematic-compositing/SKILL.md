---
name: cinematic-compositing
description: Integrate founder footage, real UI and generated scenes into a coherent cinematic film.
---

Build the edit around a single narrative question and a demonstrable payoff. Match framing, scale, lighting, contrast, color temperature, shadows and texture across founder, product and scene layers. Use transitions that preserve an object, gesture, camera direction or information relationship. Keep typography, easing and alignment consistent. Preserve deliberate pauses where a viewer needs to read or understand a result.

Use clean source masters and correct alpha/mattes where available. Prefer actual captures for UI and exact data graphics; use generated scenes for illustrative context. Avoid unnecessary upscaling of preview files and retain clean versions before adding grain or grade. Keep synthetic/demo material labelled in provenance and visibly identified where it could be mistaken for genuine evidence.

Review moving footage as well as representative stills. Edge artifacts, frame pacing, color shifts and blurred type may be absent in a single attractive frame. Finish variants with an intentional crop and separate subtitle/master exports.
