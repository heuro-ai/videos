---
name: spatial-ui-animation
description: Make legible spatial interfaces, parcel layers and immersive motion for product films.
---

Start with the relationship the viewer must understand. Use CSS perspective for simple panels, Three.js/React Three Fiber for genuine 3D objects, and a suitable geographic engine for maps/terrain. Choose coherent depth, camera lens, focal plane, lighting and occlusion. Animate one causal relationship at a time; preserve text legibility and orientation as the camera moves.

Use frame-driven, deterministic animation in offline renders. In Remotion follow the acquired official markup, timing and map guidance; do not rely on CSS keyframes or wall-clock animation for rendered motion. Keep the camera and scene timing reproducible.

For geographic content preserve coordinate systems and source provenance. Use real parcel boundaries only from an authorized source, and keep illustrative geometry distinguishable from site evidence. Credit required map/imagery sources in the delivery. Do not infer ownership, legal feasibility or implemented integrations from a visually convincing scene.
