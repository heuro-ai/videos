Read the supplied private persona, product context, preferences and live integrations when available. Unknowns remain unknown. Follow workflows/production.md and workflows/providers.md. Use video-strategy for the brief/script, character-continuity for own identity, storyboard-director for shots, edit-and-localize for post-production and video-quality for review.

Use scripts/studio.py to initialize a project, inspect gaps, generate prompts and retain provider handles. The CLI does not execute remote jobs. Check live connected tools before submission, preserve returned handles, and resume existing jobs. Use scripts/assemble.py for supplied local footage and scripts/video.py for probing and aspect exports.

Source all product claims. Distinguish illustrative/generated imagery from genuine evidence. A name alone is not a verified avatar/voice. Reuse known preferences; do not require reconfirmation of already authorized work. Publishing and spending follow actual user instructions. Keep private context, job IDs and generated packs out of a public repository.
