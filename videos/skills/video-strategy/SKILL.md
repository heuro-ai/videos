---
name: video-strategy
description: Translate a video request and established creator/product context into a focused brief, hooks and substantiated script.
---

Read the private persona, product context, preferences and source register if supplied. Separate personal content from product marketing. Select one audience, one useful message and one desired action. Reuse known language, tone and platform preferences; keep missing choices explicit. Draft three distinct hooks and one timed script with a clear payoff. Verify product claims against current evidence; do not turn a strategy proposal into a delivered feature or invent a creator's personal experience. Hand the script and claim sources to storyboard-director.
