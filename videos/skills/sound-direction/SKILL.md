---
name: sound-direction
description: Design voice, music and sound effects around the film's narrative and motion.
---

Create a sound cue sheet from the storyboard: dialogue, pauses, ambience, music entries and purposeful motion accents. Use the creator's verified own voice or authorized supplied recordings. Proofread pronunciation and preserve natural delivery; a voice name alone is not proof of selectable identity. Resolve missing access before generating a substitute.

Choose licensed music and effects suited to the emotional arc. Record source/rights and usage limits. Mix dialogue first, then music/ambience/effects around it. Check intelligibility, clipping, abrupt ambience changes and playback on headphones and phone speakers. Use appropriate FFmpeg loudness analysis/normalization or a finishing editor; record actual measurements rather than claiming a loudness target was achieved without checking.

Keep music genre, intensity and final loudness aligned with the brief and delivery platform. A synthetic technical test tone is not a music or voice-quality test. Deliver clean dialogue, mixed master and stems when requested so revisions do not require rebuilding the entire soundtrack.
