name: video-quality
description: Review a finished video for creator identity, product accuracy, audiovisual quality and delivery requirements.
---

Watch the full video. Inspect representative frames and all transitions, speaker mouth sync, names, captions, UI labels and any maps. Compare the result to the brief and approved reference traits. Check ownership and provenance of media. Use ffprobe for duration, dimensions, frame rate and audio presence; listen for clipping and speech masking. Confirm aspect variants on a phone. A successful encode is only a technical check. Record unresolved creative issues and the master/subtitle/thumbnail/storage locations before reporting completion. Follow the user's actual publication instructions.
